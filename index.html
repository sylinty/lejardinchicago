About Le Jardin

Food, family and a place to gather.

Le Jardin is a Chicago restaurant, tavern and banquet hall centered on Cameroon-French fusion and warm hospitality.

Our identity

Cameroon-French fusion at the heart of it.

The menu brings Cameroonian traditions together with French culinary influence, generous grilled favorites and plates made for sharing and celebrating.

Le Jardin is also a tavern and banquet hall — a place where meals, drinks and important moments can happen under the same roof.
