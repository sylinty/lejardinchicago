About Le Jardin

Food, family and a place to gather.

Le Jardin is a Chicago restaurant and banquet hall with Cameroonian roots and a warm, celebratory spirit.

Our identity

Cameroonian flavor at the heart of it.

The menu is rooted in Cameroonian dishes while making room for grilled favorites, sides and plates designed for sharing and celebrating.

Le Jardin is also a banquet hall — a place where meals and important moments can happen under the same roof.
